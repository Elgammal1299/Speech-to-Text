# Privacy Policy for Voice Notes - Speech to Text

**Last Updated: December 12, 2024**

## Introduction

Voice Notes - Speech to Text ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles your information.

## Information Collection and Use

### Data We Collect

**Voice Recordings:**
- The app records audio through your device's microphone when you explicitly press the record button
- All voice recordings are stored **locally on your device only**
- We do NOT upload, transmit, or share your voice recordings with any third parties
- We do NOT have access to your recordings

**App Preferences:**
- Settings such as theme preference (dark/light mode) and auto-save settings
- These preferences are stored locally using Android SharedPreferences
- No personal information is collected

**Speech-to-Text Data (Optional Feature):**
- When you enable the speech-to-text feature, your voice is processed to convert it to text
- This processing may use your device's built-in speech recognition or Android's Speech Recognition API
- The transcribed text is stored locally on your device
- We do NOT store or transmit the audio or text to our servers

### Data We Do NOT Collect

- We do NOT collect personal information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
- We do NOT collect device identifiers
- We do NOT use analytics or tracking tools
- We do NOT share data with third parties
- We do NOT serve advertisements

## Permissions

The app requires the following permissions:

**RECORD_AUDIO (Microphone):**
- **Purpose:** Required to record voice notes
- **Usage:** Only used when you actively press the record button
- **Data Storage:** Audio files are stored locally in the app's private storage directory
- **Data Sharing:** None - recordings never leave your device

**INTERNET (Optional):**
- **Purpose:** May be used for speech-to-text functionality if device's built-in recognition requires it
- **Usage:** Only when speech-to-text feature is enabled
- **Data Sharing:** Only with Android's Speech Recognition API (Google's service) if enabled

**WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (If applicable):**
- **Purpose:** To allow exporting notes to your device storage
- **Usage:** Only when you explicitly choose to export notes
- **Data Sharing:** Files are saved to your chosen location on your device

## Data Storage and Security

- All data is stored locally on your device using Android's secure storage mechanisms
- Voice recordings are saved in the app's private directory, inaccessible to other apps
- App preferences are stored using Android's SharedPreferences API
- When you uninstall the app, all data is automatically deleted from your device

## Data Deletion

You have complete control over your data:

- **Individual Notes:** Delete any voice note by swiping left/right in the app
- **All Data:** Use the "Clear All Data" option in Settings to delete all notes and recordings
- **Uninstall:** Uninstalling the app removes all associated data from your device

## Children's Privacy

Voice Notes - Speech to Text does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. The app is suitable for all ages as it only stores data locally on the device.

## Third-Party Services

The app does NOT integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

**Optional Third-Party Services:**
- **Android Speech Recognition:** If you enable speech-to-text, the app may use Android's built-in speech recognition service, which is provided by Google. Google's privacy policy applies to this service: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

## Data Transfer and International Users

Since all data is stored locally on your device, there are no international data transfers.

- **Right to Deletion:** You can delete all data at any time through the app settings
- **Right to Portability:** You can export your notes using the export feature
- **No Sale of Data:** We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information
